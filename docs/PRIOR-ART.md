# Prior art - searched 2026-11-10, before registering this mod

The check-precedent rule: search Nexus and GitHub for a mod that already does this.

## Nexus (Skyrim Special Edition, searched by houseCARL's Nexus search)
Queries: "nordic carved weapons", "nordic weapons replacer", "nordic weapons".
- Resurgence Armory - Nordic Carved Weapons and Armor Overhaul (id 69934, Iconic): a mesh overhaul and retexture of the
  same Dragonborn Nordic set. The closest existing mod. Different art (its own meshes and textures), vanilla-path replacer.
- Runed Nordic Weapons (id 11559) and its Replacer Version (id 36253): retextures with glowing runes; not new models.
- Nordic Armors and Weapons - Environment Map Edit (id 115987): shader edit only.
- Simply Nordic Draugr and Hero Weapons (id 49306): the Ancient Nord / Nord Hero weapons, not the Dragonborn Nordic set.
- Better Nordic Weapons (17817), Nordic Weapons (62014), Nordic Roamer (98401), Nordic Weapons and Carved Armor in
  Skyrim (180171): standalone new weapons or armour, not replacers of this set.
None ships new from-scratch models with carved knotwork in a custom TruePBR texture, swapped per record through
SkyPatcher so other mesh replacers of the vanilla paths are not overwritten.
